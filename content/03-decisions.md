This is a sample post, not a description of Matthew's professional experience.

## Give the decision context

Describe the constraints, the people involved, and the outcome you were trying to achieve.

## Make the trade-offs visible

Use this space to explain which alternatives you considered and why one approach fit the situation.

## Reflect on the outcome

What happened? What surprised you? What would you do differently next time?
