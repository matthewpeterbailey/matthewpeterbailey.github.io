[Read the original article in the *European Journal of Applied Mathematics*](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0956792518000190).

This paper reframed the two-process model of sleep-wake regulation as a one-dimensional circle map with gaps. That perspective makes it possible to study how changes in the system create and alter periodic sleep patterns.

The result is a richer account of the model’s bifurcations, including how border collisions extend the familiar saddle-node structure of continuous circle maps.
