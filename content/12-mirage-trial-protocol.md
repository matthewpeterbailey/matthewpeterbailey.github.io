[Read the original protocol in *BMJ Open*](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-060498).

The MIRAGE protocol set out a UK multi-centre pilot randomised trial of functional imagery training plus usual care, compared with usual care alone, for people with alcohol dependence and alcohol-related liver disease.

It focused on an important feasibility question: can this kind of psychologically informed support be delivered by alcohol liaison nurses in and after an inpatient admission, with enough recruitment, intervention fidelity and follow-up to justify a larger trial?
