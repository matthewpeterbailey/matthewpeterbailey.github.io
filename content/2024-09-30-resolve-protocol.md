[Read the original protocol in *Pilot and Feasibility Studies*](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40814-024-01544-x).

RESOLVE is a feasibility trial for people with hepatic steatosis who are due to have liver resection. It compares a two-week very low-calorie diet with usual treatment, building on the use of pre-operative dietary interventions in other surgical settings.

Before asking whether the intervention improves outcomes, the study asks whether a full trial can recruit, retain and support participants safely and consistently across UK centres.
