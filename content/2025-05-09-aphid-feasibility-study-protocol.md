[Read the original article in *Frontiers in Health Services*](https://doi.org/10.3389/frhs.2025.1393805).

The APHID feasibility study explores a consistent and understandable way to measure the burden of antipsychotic prescribing for adults supported by specialist intellectual disability services in England and Wales. It aims to make variation visible across services and over time, helping clinicians, researchers and policy-makers examine the factors associated with overprescribing.

It is a good example of measurement work having a practical purpose: turning complex prescribing data into evidence people can use to ask better questions and target improvement.
