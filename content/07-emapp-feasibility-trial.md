[Read the original article in *Pilot and Feasibility Studies*](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40814-025-01638-0).

EMaPP examined whether a larger randomised trial could test customised dynamic elastomeric fabric orthoses alongside standard advice and exercise for women with chronic pelvic girdle pain after pregnancy.

The work combined clinical, qualitative and economic perspectives. It found that recruitment was the central challenge, despite sustained efforts across several sites, giving the team useful evidence about what a future trial would need to do differently.
