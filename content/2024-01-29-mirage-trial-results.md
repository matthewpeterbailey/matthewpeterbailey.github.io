[Read the original results in *BMJ Open Gastroenterology*](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgast-2023-001267).

MIRAGE tested the practicalities of delivering functional imagery training alongside usual care for people admitted to hospital with alcohol use disorder and alcohol-related liver disease. The intervention uses imagery to strengthen motivation, rehearse plans and work through barriers to change.

As a pilot trial, the emphasis was on whether the methods could support a future definitive study: recruitment, delivery, follow-up and the proposed economic evaluation all mattered as much as the clinical outcomes.
