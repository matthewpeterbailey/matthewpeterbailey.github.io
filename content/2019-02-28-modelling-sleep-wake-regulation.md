[Read the thesis in Surrey Open Research](https://doi.org/10.15126/thesis.00850423).

My PhD examined the two-process model of sleep regulation: a mathematical model that describes sleep-wake cycles through the interaction of circadian and homeostatic processes.

The work explored the model’s dynamical structure, including changes between one sleep period and multiple sleep periods, and used modified versions of the model to investigate sleep timing and duration across the lifespan.
