[Read the original protocol in *BMJ Open*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12035433/).

Anxiety can have a substantial effect on independence and quality of life for people with Parkinson’s. OBtAIN-PD tests the feasibility of an occupation-based intervention that focuses on meaningful everyday activity, alongside usual occupational therapy care.

The study is designed to establish whether the intervention, its delivery and its outcome measures can support a future definitive trial. Its co-production approach is especially important: the intervention was shaped with people with Parkinson’s, care partners and clinicians.
