[Read the original poster record from the University of Plymouth](https://pearl.plymouth.ac.uk/pms-research/1332/).

This work considers how clinical trial systems can move beyond paper-heavy validation processes without losing the traceability and assurance that Good Clinical Practice requires.

The core argument is familiar to software engineers: version control, cloud environments and automated build and deployment pipelines can make evidence of change, review and approval clearer. The challenge is ensuring that electronic records remain accessible and inspectable when they matter most.
